---
title: Contact Details
description: The business's phone, mobile, email or address, taken from Site Details.
---
## When to use

- Showing how to reach the business on a page, such as a contact, location or about page.
- Keeping details the same everywhere: they come from Site Details, so a change there updates every page that shows them.
- Tick only the details that suit the page, such as just the phone and email under a short invitation to get in touch.

## When not to use

- Contact details beside a form: switch on Include contact details in the Form block.
- Someone else's details, such as a staff member's direct line or another organisation's number: write them in the text editor, because Site Details holds only the business's own details.
- A prompt to get in touch as its own section: use Call to action with a button to the contact page.

## Notes for AI

- Use this set wherever the business's contact details turn up in content you are writing or bringing across, rather than typing them: a contact section, numbers listed under a heading, or the details at the end of a policy.
- A detail inside a sentence, such as "call us on 0800 123 456 to book", can't hold the set. End the sentence before the detail and put the set after it, such as "Call us to book:" followed by the set with Phone ticked. If the sentence can't be reworded without changing what it says, leave it as it is and tell the person that its number won't follow Site Details.
- If a detail in the content differs from the one in Site Details, ask which is right before using the set. Don't change Site Details on your own, and don't type the other version into the page.
- A contact detail the business has that Site Details doesn't hold yet, such as a postal address, a second phone number or a fax, gets its own field in Site Details and its own option in this set, and then the set shows it. That changes how the site is built: if you are building the site, follow Site Details global in the build recipe. Otherwise ask the developer, and keep the detail typed out until the option exists.
- Before adding the set, check that Site Details has a value for each detail you tick. An empty detail shows nothing, and with no values the set shows nothing at all. The address counts as filled when either its street address or Address shown on the website has a value.
- Change a detail only in Site Details, and only when the person asks, because the change reaches every page that shows it. Never type the details into the page instead.
- Phone, mobile and email show as links that start a call or an email. The address shows as text with its line breaks.
- The details show one below another, in the order they are listed in Display Contacts.
- The set has no heading, so put a short heading or sentence before it, such as "Get in touch".
- It suits a Text block, or the narrow column of Text Columns beside a map or opening hours.
