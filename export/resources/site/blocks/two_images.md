---
title: Two Images
description: A heading and optional text with two images, laid out side by side, stacked or offset.
---
## When to use

- Showing two related photos together, such as before and after, a space and a detail, or two products.
- A short introduction that two images help tell.
- Choose Display style for the layout: Standard puts the text above both images, Inline sets the text beside them in a row of three, Stacked puts both images on one side of the text, and Offset pairs one tall image with a smaller one below the text.

## When not to use

- One image beside text: use Media and text.
- Three or more photos: use Gallery.
- Several items that each need their own title: use Cards.
- Long text: use a "simple" page or post instead.

## Notes for AI

- Give both images alt text, and choose images that work as a pair.
- Keep the text short: a heading, an optional subheading and up to about 80 words. With Inline the text column is a third of the width, so keep it to about 40 words.
- Use the images' Crop options so both images share a shape, unless the layout is Offset.
- With Offset, the tall image is cropped to fill its column, so use a photo whose subject sits in the middle.
- With Offset, captions sit over the images, so keep them to a few words.
- Text position chooses the side the text sits on for Inline, Stacked and Offset. It has no effect on Standard.
