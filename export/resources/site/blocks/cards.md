---
title: Cards
description: A row of cards, each with a title, short text, an optional image or icon and an optional button.
---
## When to use

- Several short items of equal weight, such as features, benefits, steps in a process or links to key pages.
- Signposting to other pages, with one button on each card.
- Choose Card Type for the whole block: Text for bordered cards, Image for a photo above each title, Icon for a small icon or logo.

## When not to use

- One item on its own: use Media and text or Call to action.
- Two pieces of longer text: use Text Columns.
- A set of photos with no text: use Gallery.
- Items that need headings or more than a short paragraph each: use Text Columns or separate Text blocks.
- Bullet points or lists in the card text. Keep it to a sentence or two. When every card needs a short list, such as what a service includes, ask for a card style with a list of its own, which suits five or six points. More than about seven makes the cards too tall and uneven, so use Text Columns or another layout instead.

## Notes for AI

- When the cards would link to entries in a collection, such as posts, projects or team members, look for a block that lists that collection, usually listed under Dynamic in the page builder, and use it instead of Cards. Use Cards for collection entries only when there is a very good reason.
- Use three cards, or six in two rows of three. Two or four cards form two columns, and one card sits alone in the middle.
- Keep every card alike: a title of a few words, and card text of one or two sentences, about 30 words, with no headings, bullets or lists. Card Text is a text editor, and a list in it makes one card taller than the rest.
- When the cards need a list of points, add a card style for it rather than putting the list in Card Text. That is a change to this block, so it goes in the plan: a grid with one text field, one row per point, shown as a list on the card. Aim for five or six points on every card. More than about seven makes a card too tall, or the cards uneven from one to the next, so consider a different layout, such as Text Columns.
- Each card shows only its first button, and that button makes the whole card clickable. Give each card one button at most.
- With Card Type set to Image or Icon, every card needs an image. Use the same image shape for all cards in a block.
- Write card titles and text to a similar length, so the cards line up evenly.
